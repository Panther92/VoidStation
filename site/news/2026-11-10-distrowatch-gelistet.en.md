# VoidStation is listed on DistroWatch

On 5 October, VoidStation went on the [DistroWatch waiting list](/en/news/distrowatch/). As of today it is officially in the database: **[VoidStation on DistroWatch](https://distrowatch.com/table.php?distribution=voidstation)**.

We expected a wait of about a year. It turned out to be just over a month. The entry shows the 2026.11 live ISO with a screenshot, package list and download, right next to the big distributions.

## How you can help {#help}

- **Rate it:** The DistroWatch page has no rating yet. If you use VoidStation, click "Rate this project" there and write a few sentences about what works well and what doesn't.
- **Drop by:** DistroWatch ranks projects by visits to their page. Every visit counts.
- **Spread the word:** Know someone with an old PC that would still make a good TV computer? The [live ISO](seite:download) is the easiest way to try it.

Thanks to Ladislav Bodnar and the DistroWatch team for the evaluation, and to everyone who has tried VoidStation, reported bugs and recommended it. Without you, this wouldn't have happened so fast.
